Unsubscribe from Caml-list

25 Dec, 2025 [howto] 1 min read

  1. Goto sympa.inria.fr
  2. Click the big blue search button like in Fig. Unsub-1, then enter caml-list
  3. Do unsubscribe
  4. Enter email address
  5. Goto email like in Fig. Unsub-2, then click the link
  6. Confirm un-subscription
